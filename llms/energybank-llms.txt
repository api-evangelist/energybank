# EnergyBank

> EnergyBank is a New Zealand deep-tech energy company building next-generation energy
> storage for the offshore wind market. It firms floating offshore wind generation at the
> turbine so compute modules can be co-located with generation — "data to the power rather
> than electricity to the data" — removing the grid connection, enabling consentless
> high-seas deployment, and allowing direct ocean cooling of the co-located compute.

EnergyBank publishes **no public API**, developer portal, SDK, CLI, or machine-readable
specification. It is a hardware and energy-infrastructure company, not an API provider.
Probes on 2026-07-20 found no `/.well-known/` documents, no `/docs`, `/api`, `/developers`,
`/status`, or `/llms.txt`. Its GitHub account (`energybank-nz`) has zero public repositories.
